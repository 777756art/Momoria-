Memoria was born from a simple belief: the best gifts aren't things — they're memories. Founded in 2017 in a small Indiranagar studio, we started with a single laser engraver, a lot of heart, and the dream of making personalized gifting accessible for every occasion.

Today, we're a team of 12 passionate artisans crafting thousands of bespoke gifts every year — from memory frames to constellation maps — all by hand, all with care.
